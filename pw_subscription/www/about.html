Our mission: deliver business software that actually works

{{ company_name }} is a software company that designs, builds, and operates business software across multiple domains. We implement and customise ERPNext for businesses of all sizes — accounting, inventory, HR, payroll, and manufacturing — and build purpose-built SaaS applications where generic tools don't fit.

Our flagship SaaS product, CureDesk, manages the complete patient journey for clinics and hospitals — from walk-in registration and QR check-in, through prescription writing and lab orders, to invoice generation, POS reconciliation, and ERPNext journal entries. Inpatient management, telemedicine, multi-lingual prescriptions, and self-booking appointments complete the picture.

We believe software should be fast, reliable, and intuitive — whether it's an accounting system, a clinic platform, or a custom Frappe module. That belief drives everything we build.
