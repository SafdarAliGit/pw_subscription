FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about CureDesk. Can't find the answer? Reach out to us.

What happens after the free trial ends?
After your {{ trial_days }}-day free trial, you receive a {{ grace_period }} grace period with on-screen warnings before the app is locked. Transfer the monthly fee and share your screenshot — we reactivate access within a few hours.
Can I use the app on multiple devices?
Yes — the app is fully web-based. Access it simultaneously from any desktop, tablet, or mobile device using your clinic's unique URL and login credentials. No installation required.
Is my patient data safe and private?
Absolutely. Every clinic runs on its own dedicated database instance — your records are never co-mingled with other clinics. You have 100% ownership and can request a full data export at any time.
How quickly is my subscription activated?
We typically activate within a few hours of receiving payment confirmation on business days. Send your bank transfer screenshot via WhatsApp for the fastest response.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes. There are no long-term contracts and no cancellation fees. Subscriptions are month-to-month — stop paying whenever you like. Your access continues until the end of your paid period.
Is there a per-doctor or per-user fee?
No. The flat monthly fee covers the entire clinic — unlimited doctors, staff accounts, patients, and visits. You will never be charged extra for adding more users or growing your practice.
What prescription languages are supported?
Prescriptions can be printed in English, Urdu, and Arabic. The language is selectable per-prescription, making it easy to serve patients from different linguistic backgrounds in one clinic.

Our team is here to help. Reach out and we'll get back to you the same day.
