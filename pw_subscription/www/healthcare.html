Platform Features

12 Modules. One Complete Platform.

Every workflow your clinic needs — clinical care, pharmacy, lab, appointments, billing, and accounting — unified under a single roof.

🧑‍⚕️
Patient & Queue
  • QR / barcode scan check-in
  • Walk-in registration with vitals
  • Live token queue with real-time updates
  • Visit history & patient search
  • Allergies, emergency contacts, family history
  • Queue stats: total / completed / pending
📝
Prescription Writing
  • Medicine with dosage, frequency & duration
  • Lab test & imaging recommendations
  • Diagnosis taxonomy (categorised)
  • Instruction library + custom notes
  • Follow-up scheduling & reminders
  • Branded print with doctor license
💊
Medicines & Inventory
  • Formulary with brand & generic search
  • Multi-language dose labels (EN / UR / AR)
  • Real-time stock lookup & management
  • Bulk CSV import
  • Categories, companies, dose units
  • Data manager (enable / disable / price)
🔬
Lab & Diagnostics
  • Test order from prescription
  • Parameterised result entry with ranges
  • Critical value flagging
  • Lab invoice generation
  • Result templates per test
  • Bulk CSV import for tests
🧾
Invoicing & Payments
  • OPD, medicine & general invoices
  • Sales Invoice auto-creation
  • Payment entry (Cash / Card / Cheque / Online)
  • Credit notes & cancellation
  • POS session open / close with reconciliation
  • Invoice status tracking & history
📅
Appointments
  • Patient self-booking portal
  • Doctor schedule & time-slot management
  • Holiday & leave blocking
  • Accept / reject with auto-refund
  • Pre-visit token on acceptance
  • Appointment status workflow
🏥
IPD – Inpatient
  • Ward / floor / room / bed management
  • Admission, transfer & discharge
  • Prescription rounds & diet orders
  • Procedure scheduling with OR conflict check
  • Consent forms & clearance checklist
  • Discharge summary PDF export
🛒
Purchase & Expenses
  • Purchase orders & invoices
  • Supplier management
  • Purchase return (Debit Note)
  • Expense entry with GL journal posting
  • Recurring expense templates
  • Daily / monthly expense summary
💻
Online Visit (Telemedicine)
  • Guest self-service request (no login)
  • Multi-provider video: Jitsi, Zoom, Meet, WhatsApp
  • Symptom & medication history capture
  • Stripe, PayPal, JazzCash, EasyPaisa payments
  • Staff accept / reject / complete workflow
  • Automatic & manual refund handling
  • Real-time new-request notifications to staff
  • Visit token tracking for patient status check
📊
Analytics & Dashboard
  • Revenue trends & daily visit stats
  • POS session performance metrics
  • Expense summary by category
  • Ward admissions report
  • Queue & control-board view
  • Extensible via PW Revenue Source
💰
Financial Accounting
  • Auto Sales Invoice from prescription
  • POS Invoice for point-of-sale
  • Payment & journal entry creation
  • Chart of accounts & ledger management
  • Daily / monthly financial reports
  • Per-clinic isolated accounting books
⚙️
Settings & Multi-lingual
  • Clinic branding, logo & stamp
  • English, Urdu & Arabic UI + prescriptions
  • Role-based page access control
  • Holiday, fee & account configuration
  • Service packages & room types
  • Doctor & staff profile management

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about {{ app_name }}. Can't find the answer? Reach out to us.

What happens after the free trial ends?
After your {{ trial_days }}-day free trial, you receive a grace period with on-screen warnings before the app is locked. Transfer the monthly fee and share your screenshot — we reactivate access within a few hours.
Can I use the app on multiple devices?
Yes — the app is fully web-based. Access it simultaneously from any desktop, tablet, or mobile device using your clinic's unique URL and login credentials. No installation required.
Is my patient data safe and private?
Absolutely. Every clinic runs on its own dedicated database instance — your records are never co-mingled with other clinics. You have 100% ownership and can request a full data export at any time.
How quickly is my subscription activated?
We typically activate within a few hours of receiving payment confirmation on business days. Send your bank transfer screenshot via WhatsApp for the fastest response.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes. There are no long-term contracts and no cancellation fees. Subscriptions are month-to-month — stop paying whenever you like. Your access continues until the end of your paid period.
Is there a per-doctor or per-user fee?
No. The flat monthly fee covers the entire clinic — unlimited doctors, staff accounts, patients, and visits. You will never be charged extra for adding more users or growing your practice.
What prescription languages are supported?
Prescriptions can be printed in English, Urdu, and Arabic. The language is selectable per-prescription, making it easy to serve patients from different linguistic backgrounds in one clinic.
